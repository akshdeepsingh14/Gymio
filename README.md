# Gymio — Gym Management, Simplified

**One platform. Your entire gym. Zero unnecessary complexity.**

Gymio is a modern gym management platform designed to help gym owners manage their members, memberships, renewals, payments, receipts, and everyday operations from one place.

Built with a focus on simplicity, accessibility, and premium design, Gymio aims to replace scattered spreadsheets, paper records, and manual administrative work with a streamlined digital experience.

**Our vision:** Make professional gym management accessible to every gym owner, with all core features included and no feature-based paywalls.

---

## About Gymio

Running a gym involves more than providing equipment and training. Owners must track memberships, monitor expiry dates, record payments, issue receipts, manage member requests, and keep members informed.

Gymio brings these essential activities together in one organized workspace.

Whether you're managing a local fitness studio or a growing gym, Gymio is being designed to make daily operations easier so you can spend more time focusing on your members and your business.

## What Gymio Offers

### Member Management
- Maintain organized member records.
- Add, edit, search, and filter members.
- Track membership status and expiry dates.
- View individual membership and payment histories.

### Memberships & Renewals
- Create and manage membership plans.
- Support weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly, and custom durations.
- Track upcoming renewals and expired memberships.
- Configure renewal reminders and grace-period policies.

### Payments & Digital Receipts
- Record payments made through UPI, cash, and other configured methods.
- Maintain payment histories.
- Generate receipts with member, membership, and payment details.
- Filter payment records and prepare for printable or downloadable receipts.

*Manually recording a payment does not independently verify that a transaction was completed.*

### Member Access & Approvals
- Provide a separate member portal.
- Allow members to request access to their gym accounts.
- Give gym owners an approval or rejection workflow.
- Keep owner management screens separate from member experiences.

### Gym Updates & Announcements
- Publish gym announcements.
- Share gym photos and updates.
- Keep members informed about important news and activities.

### Dashboard & Analytics
- View membership and revenue summaries.
- Monitor active and inactive members.
- Identify upcoming renewals and pending access requests.
- Review payment activity and membership trends.

### Gym Branding & Settings
- Configure gym information and branding.
- Customize appearance preferences.
- Support dark and light themes.
- Configure relevant membership, notification, and receipt preferences.

## Designed for Two Experiences

### 1. Gym Owner Portal

A centralized workspace for managing the business side of a gym.

Owners are intended to manage members, membership plans, payments, renewals, receipts, access requests, announcements, and gym settings through a responsive dashboard.

### 2. Gym Member Portal

A dedicated experience where approved members can access their own gym information.

The planned member portal includes membership details, expiry dates, payment history, receipts, announcements, and profile settings.

Member access and protected information will require proper server-side authentication and authorization in the backend implementation.

## What Makes Gymio Different?

- **All-features-included philosophy:** Core gym management capabilities are intended to be available to every gym owner without feature-based pricing tiers.
- **Simple workflows:** Common tasks should take fewer steps.
- **Premium interface:** A modern fitness-tech aesthetic with charcoal backgrounds, lime accents, polished typography, and clean layouts.
- **Responsive by design:** Built to support desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and mobile devices.
- **Owner and member experiences:** Separate interfaces for gym administration and member self-service.
- **Built for growth:** An architecture intended to support future backend integration without rebuilding the entire frontend.
